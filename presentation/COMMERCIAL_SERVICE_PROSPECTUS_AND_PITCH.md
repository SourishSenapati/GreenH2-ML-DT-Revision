## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

Electrolyzer stack replacement dominates the Levelized Cost of Hydrogen (LCOH). Today, industrial multi-megawatt Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM) electrolyzers suffer from:
1. **Unplanned Catalyst Degradation:** Intermittent renewable power causes rapid Ostwald ripening of scarce Iridium ($\text{IrO}_2$) and Platinum catalysts.
2. **Dielectric Biofilm Fouling:** Oligocarbotolerant bacterial biofilms (*Pseudomonas spp.*) colonize porous transport layers, causing parasitic ohmic spikes ($+58.8\ \text{mV}$) that operators mistake for catastrophic membrane ruptures.
3. **Destructive Teardowns & Chemical Biocides:** Operators prematurely flush stacks with toxic chemical biocides (glutaraldehyde, sodium hypochlorite) that poison membranes and shorten cell life.

### The AERO-H2 Solution:
Our proprietary **Physics-Informed Bayesian Transformer (PIBT)** digital twin runs 24/7 on industrial PLCs or edge processors, continuously decoupling electrochemical decay from biological biofilm fouling without cell dismounting.
