Unaddressed Plant Reliability Risk: In-Operando Dielectric Biofouling in Ultra-Pure DI Water Loops

Standard engineering procurement specifications mandate feed water compliance with ASTM D1193 Type 1 (>18.2 MΩ·cm), assuming water chemistry is static. In continuous commercial operation, closed DI water loops foster dielectric extracellular polymeric substance (EPS) biofilms within 600–1,200 operating hours. Conventional SCADA systems cannot differentiate this dielectric resistance spike from electrochemical catalyst degradation, prompting unwarranted MEA condemnations. HYDROSENSE's decoupled Bayesian impedance observer identifies EPS biofilms in <1.4 seconds, commanding an automated pure DI surge that restores cell conductivity without chemical biocides or plant shutdowns.
