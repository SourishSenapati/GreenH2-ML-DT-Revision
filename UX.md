# UX: User Journeys, Interaction Principles & Design System

## 1. Design System Tokens (Pure Light Mode Architectural Drafting)
- **Backgrounds**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` drafting canvas, light technical wash `#F8FAFC`.
- **Borders & Hairlines**: Slate 200 `#E2E8F0` and Slate 300 `#CBD5E1`. Zero fuzzy dropshadows; clean, precision 1px borders.
- **Accents**: Technical Cobalt `#0284C7` (primary brand), Precision Emerald `#059669` (passing safety & verified status), Industrial Amber `#D97706` (warning/operating notices).
- **Typography**: Inter (400, 500, 600, 700, 800) for clean semantic hierarchy; JetBrains Mono for part codes, tag references, and engineering coordinates.

## 2. Interaction Principles
1. **Zero Fake Claims / Immediate Traceability**: Clicking on any credential or certification badge immediately opens the exact mathematical derivation, governing standard clause, and executable calculation code.
2. **Deep Component-Level 3D Interactivity**:
   - 3D Plant Model: Drag to orbit, right-click to pan, scroll to zoom.
   - Explode View Slider: Explodes the 11 subsystems radially outward to inspect internal stack arrays, knockout drum internals, and compressor skids.
   - Click-to-Inspect Raycasting: Clicking any 3D asset highlights it in technical cyan, isolates it, and updates the right-hand engineering HUD.
3. **Live Oscilloscope & Digital Twin Simulator**:
   - Clean, light-mode HTML5 canvas tracking true Butler-Volmer overpotentials.
   - One-click "Simulate Biofilm Fouling" trigger to observe the exact 1.38s trip threshold, dielectric resistance jump, and autonomous pure DI water flush.
4. **Keyboard Accessibility**:
   - Modal lightboxes and 15-slide master deck support Escape, Left Arrow, and Right Arrow key navigation.
   - Interactive calculators update immediately on slider drag with full numerical input feedback.
